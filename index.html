How scoring works

+3

Full row or column. Complete all 7 tiles in a row or a column for 3 bonus points each.

+10

Full diagonal. Complete all 7 tiles on a diagonal for 10 bonus points. There are two diagonals.

3 skips

Per team. Every team starts with 3 skips available. All three teams are at 0 used so far.

Tile points run from 3 to 7 — the seven XP tiles across the top row are worth 7 each, and the board holds 208 tile points in total before line bonuses. A full board with every row, column and diagonal would add 7×3 + 7×3 + 2×10 = 62 bonus points. Proof screenshots stay in the Raven's Watch Discord, same as now.
